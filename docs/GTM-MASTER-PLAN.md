## 0. The role and the steelman (why this plan wins)

Run this like the CMO of a category insurgent, not a generalist startup. The closest playbooks to borrow from:
**Linear / Stripe-early** (product-led, taste, founder voice, docs-as-marketing), **Toast / Square** (vertical SaaS earning
SMB trust through education + local presence), **Pieter Levels / indie hackers** (solo-founder, build-in-public, self-serve),
and **PointClickCare / Epic** as the *incumbents to out-manoeuvre* in regulated care (slow, generic, enterprise-only).

**The steelman.** AI has driven the marginal cost of plausible content, landing pages, and cold outreach to zero. The market
is drowning in slop, and Day Zero's buyers (skeptical operators carrying personal accountability) are the most slop-fatigued of
all. When plausibility is free, plausibility stops being a signal. What still carries signal is what AI cannot cheaply fake:
lived domain expertise, a named accountable founder, honesty against self-interest, craft, and real relationships. A small,
founder-led, regulation-deep studio is **structurally advantaged** here, over both the AI-wrapper crowd (no expertise, no
accountability) and the incumbents (can't be personal or nimble). So the GTM is not "spend to be seen." It is **manufacture and
distribute authentic authority**, let the product's grounded AI do the boring verifiable work, and be unmistakably human in the
go-to-market. (Full argument: `gtm-authenticity-doctrine`.)

**What we will NOT do (learning from others' mistakes):** buy growth with ads aimed at skeptics (waste); spray thin AI content
(kills the exact trust we sell on); fabricate reviews/logos/counts (honesty is the moat); over-scope channels a solo founder
can't sustain; treat three different buyers as one audience; or ship product without distribution (great product + no reach = death).

**Day Zero (umbrella / studio):** "From Day Zero to Day One." Purpose-built operating software for regulated, low-margin
operators across healthcare and hospitality. The studio story lends credibility; each product sells in its own vertical.

| Product | One-liner | Buyer (ICP) | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NutriCare** | Dietary safety + care intelligence for healthcare (aged care AND hospitals) | DON / care manager / provider exec; hospital dietetics/clinical lead | High-trust, longer cycle, pilots, warm intros, authority |
| **RestroAI** | The operating system for independent restaurants | Owner-operator / GM / head chef | SMB self-serve trial + education-led + community |
| **QueueZero** | Order-ahead + queue-busting for food trucks & pop-ups | Truck owner-operator; market/event organiser | Prosumer self-serve + on-the-ground + viral QR loop |

trust/authority (LinkedIn + industry bodies + direct); QueueZero is visual/viral (Instagram/TikTok + markets); RestroAI sits
between (education + LinkedIn + hospitality communities, some visual).

## 4. Feature-introduction cadence (per product line)

Once a product's account exists, introduce features as **operator outcomes, one at a time**, never a feature dump (anti-slop).
Each feature post = the job it does + a real screenshot/motif + a one-line "why it matters to you." Rough sequence per product:

- **NutriCare:** allergen + IDDSI conflict at point of order -> MUST/malnutrition screening -> Standard 6 evidence/QI reporting
  -> NutriAssist (grounded AI) -> FHIR export / multi-site. (Clinical-credibility order: safety first, AI last.)
- **RestroAI:** food cost + recipe costing -> 3-way match / invoice OCR -> menu engineering -> HACCP -> predictive ordering ->
  RestroAI chat. (Money first, AI as the payoff.)
- **QueueZero:** order-ahead + live wait -> kitchen display / now-serving board -> menu-from-a-photo -> QR signage -> 0% commission
  -> event pages. (Customer magic first, then operator tooling.)

## 5. Campaign concepts (specific, not generic)

- **QueueZero - "Your market, order-ahead, one weekend, zero cost."** Pilot with a Sydney/Brisbane **market organiser**: onboard
  their trucks on-site (menu-from-photo), the event's own Instagram drives customers to the event page. The "powered by
  QueueZero" footer + trucks parked side-by-side = the viral loop. Content: TikTok/IG of a queue clearing. (This is the product
  team's own recommended wedge - use it.)
- **RestroAI - "Where's your food-cost leak?"** Drive the food-cost guide + a costing audit offer. LinkedIn + IG. The
  supplier-overbilling angle (already a published post + card) is the hook.
- **NutriCare - "Standard 6 evidence, in a week, not an annual scramble."** Direct + LinkedIn to aged-care DONs; convert the
  **Al Qassimi hospital** lead into the first named case study (with permission) to open the hospital channel.
- **Day Zero - build-in-public.** Founder narrates building three products for regulated operators; honest, specific, no hype.
  Indie Hackers / LinkedIn. Turns the solo-founder "weakness" into the trust moat.

## 6. Metrics & feedback loop (state-of-the-art = measured, not vibes)

- **North Star per product:** NutriCare = booked pilots/demos; RestroAI = free-trial starts; QueueZero = trucks live (activated).
- **Leading indicators:** site traffic + resource downloads (email capture), LinkedIn post reach/engagement, demo-form submits,
  signups by source.
- **Instrument:** GA4 (installed) + UTM every campaign link; a simple monthly dashboard (source -> signups -> activation).
- **Cadence:** monthly review - what drove signups, what got engagement, what died. Double down on the 1-2 channels that work
  per product; cut the rest. Every campaign ends with a written "what we learned."
- **The loop:** strategise -> implement -> measure -> learn -> improve -> repeat. Bias to small, reversible experiments.
